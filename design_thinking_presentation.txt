Slide 1: Title Slide
* Project Name: SelfpieBlink (Smart Grocery Application)
* Topic: Design Thinking and Innovation in Local Grocery Shopping
* Core Theme: Empowering local merchants and streamlining customer experience

Slide 2: Empathize - Understanding the User
* Identified heavy customer frustration with long checkout queues at local grocery stores.
* Recognized the difficulty for small, local merchants to modernize inventory and order management.
* Observed that many customers still prefer writing physical, handwritten grocery lists.
* Noted the massive gap between traditional local store convenience and modern quick-commerce app experiences.

Slide 3: Define - The Problem Statement
* How might we bridge the gap between traditional local grocery shopping and modern online quick-commerce?
* Essential Need: A solution that empowers small vendors without requiring expensive hardware or steep learning curves.
* Essential Need: A seamless, frictionless customer experience from list-making to store pickup.
* Primary Goal: Eliminate waiting times, improve order accuracy, and digitize local commerce.

Slide 4: Ideate - Brainstorming Solutions
* Concept: A dual-sided platform connecting local shops (Merchant Dashboard) directly with neighborhood customers (Customer App).
* Innovative Idea: Allow users to simply scan their handwritten grocery lists using AI, bypassing manual search.
* Innovative Idea: Implement a flexible  checkout & pickup scheduling  system to entirely avoid physical queues.
* Technical Idea: Utilize WebSockets for real-time inventory and order synchronization.

Slide 5: Prototype - Building SelfpieBlink
* Developed a responsive, intuitive interface inspired by leading quick-commerce apps (Blinkit-style UI).
* Integrated Advanced AI (Google Gemini) for smart processing of uploaded handwritten notes.
* Built real-time WebSocket communication for instant order updates to both merchant and customer.
* Created geolocation-based store discovery for finding nearby merchants dynamically.

Slide 6: Test - Refining the Experience
* Refined AI matching algorithms to improve recognition accuracy across various handwriting styles.
* Adjusted mobile responsiveness to ensure a seamless UI on all screen sizes, particularly Android devices.
* Optimized the checkout flow with dynamic, custom UPI QR code generation.
* Implemented an interactive, non-intrusive  Gate Pass  for quick order verification upon store exit.

Slide 7: Core Innovation 1 - AI-Powered List Processing
* Disrupts traditional e-commerce  manual search and add-to-cart  flows.
* Leverages Artificial Intelligence to instantly digitize physical, handwritten grocery lists.
* Automatically matches written items with the specific store's available inventory.
* Drastically reduces customer effort and friction during the complex grocery ordering process.

Slide 8: Core Innovation 2 - Real-Time Connected Ecosystem
* Architecture eliminates the need for constant page refreshing or manual status checks.
* Live inventory tracking prevents common frustrations like out-of-stock ordering.
* Instant order status tracking bridges the communication gap between merchant and buyer.
* Geolocation tracking calculates precise distance and optimal pickup times dynamically.

Slide 9: Impact and Value Creation
* Empowers local brick-and-mortar stores to actively compete with massive quick-commerce giants.
* Saves significant time for customers, enabling true zero-queue shopping.
* Fosters local community commerce while utilizing modern, high-end technological standards.
* Delivers a scalable, low-cost digital infrastructure tailored specifically for small businesses.

Slide 10: Future Scope & Conclusion
* Potential future integration with automated last-mile delivery partners.
* Expansion of AI capabilities for predictive grocery ordering based on historical habits.
* Conclusion: SelfpieBlink represents a highly practical, innovative application of Design Thinking to solve everyday retail challenges.
